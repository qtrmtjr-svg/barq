Frequently Asked Questions

You can easily recharge your Ooredoo balance by selecting "Recharge Ooredoo Balance" from the homepage, then entering the phone number, desired amount, and choosing your preferred payment method.

We offer multiple payment options including bank cards (Visa and Mastercard), bank transfers, prepaid recharge cards, and electronic wallets.

Yes, all transactions are encrypted with SSL technology and protected with the highest security standards. Your financial data is never stored on our servers and is processed through internationally certified payment gateways.

Select "Pay Ooredoo Bills" from the homepage, then enter the account number or phone number to view the outstanding bill. Choose your payment method and complete the transaction.

Recharging adds new balance to your prepaid line, while refill renews your current plan (data, calls, messages) when it expires or before it does.

The minimum recharge is 1 Riyal, and the maximum varies depending on the payment method used. Bank cards allow recharges up to 500 Riyals per transaction.

If the recharge doesn't arrive within 10 minutes, first verify the entered number is correct. If it is, contact customer support through the "Support" section with your transaction number.

Refunds are only possible if the recharge fails. If the recharge was successfully applied to the correct number, it cannot be reversed. If you recharged the wrong number, contact support immediately.
